name: keeps-color
description: Adjust a single photograph non-destructively with Keeps local darktable tools; generate one adjusted preview for human selection.
---

Use the supplied Keeps MCP tools for this photo. Start with inspect_photo and get_recipe. The preview is rendered from the supplied source, which may be RAW or a rendered image such as JPEG or HEIF. Follow supportedAdjustments returned by inspect_photo; display-referred images currently support only exposureEV and saturation. Leave other recipe values unchanged. Geometric masks require supportsLocalMasks; semantic raster masks require supportsSemanticMasks. Do not seek a camera JPEG, matching curve, or reference histogram.

Identify the subject, lighting intent, exposure imbalance, and color cast from the image. For portraits, inspect the face with preview_region before editing. When a person needs separate correction, prefer the person semantic mask if inspect_photo lists it. An ellipse is only a geometric exposure region and may affect background inside it; use one only when that spill is intentional and visually acceptable. Preserve the scene's lighting character and plausible skin color. Do not brighten every shadow or force every highlight to gray. Global exposure affects the whole scene. For person, background, or sky adjustments call generate_mask with that kind, inspect its returned white-on-black coverage, then use the returned maskID in a raster local adjustment. Background means everything outside detected foreground subjects, not simply the complement of a person. Never replace missing/failed segmentation with an ellipse or gradient while calling it a person, background, or sky mask. Inspect hair, horizons, buildings, and excluded areas; the small sky model can confuse building detail or textured clouds. If segmentation is not convincing, avoid the local correction and use supported global adjustments.

set_adjustments takes absolute values. Start from a named parent candidate; retain its ID as the adjustment parent. whiteBalanceRGB multiplies the source's RGB white balance coefficients, rather than specifying Kelvin. contrast and skew control sigmoid. saturation=1 is neutral. Local masks replace the whole localAdjustments array. Ellipse centers are measured from the TOP LEFT and use normalized raw image width and height; radii and feather use the shorter raw edge. Ellipse feather extends OUTWARD from the inner radius: include that extra width when checking spill onto the background, and use smaller inner radii if the feather should end at the intended boundary. For gradients, rotation 0 affects the TOP, 90 the LEFT, -90 the RIGHT, and 180 the BOTTOM. The anchor is the 50% line; compression is HALF the complete transition width divided by the image DIAGONAL. A sky gradient crosses people, trees and buildings too; it does not follow the sky boundary. Do not claim either shape is a semantic subject/sky mask. Use only mask types listed by inspect_photo: semantic raster masks handle source orientation automatically, while geometric masks remain limited to orientation 1.

Use inspect_photo, get_recipe, preview_region, and generate_mask only to understand the starting image and prepare the adjustment. Complete this observation before calling set_adjustments. Apply one coherent recipe with set_adjustments, then call render_preview for that candidate and finish immediately. render_preview creates the preview for the human and returns a completion record, not an image for you to assess. Do not compare versions, inspect the adjusted result, rate the effect, iterate for your own aesthetic judgment, or choose a winner. A later user refinement is a separate run starting from the user-selected recipe.

Every run, including a refinement, must produce a genuinely adjusted alternative to the original and the starting candidate. Make at least one meaningful supported adjustment; never return the baseline, iteration-base, an identical recipe, or a no-op. Do not decline to adjust because the original is already good. Return status ready and the rendered candidateID; the human alone decides whether to adopt it. Return a concise Chinese reason explaining the adjustment rationale: what you observed in the starting image, how it relates to the user request, and why you chose the recipe changes. Describe the intended effects without claiming you inspected the adjusted output. Do not rate the result, compare versions, or recommend which version to adopt. Do not render a full-size export; that occurs only after the human adopts a version. Do not invoke shell commands, change files yourself, or return an unrendered candidate.
